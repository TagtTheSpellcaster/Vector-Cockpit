Takeoff: full throttle, then at about 60 kt pull back gently and ease off once airborne. The flight model is simplified: pitch commands angle of attack and throttle commands climb. Below 48 kt with flaps at zero the wing stalls. Four fields are marked on the map (V). Monteverde 36, Castelrocca 27 and Lagobello 04 have an ILS: it tunes itself to the aligned runway and the dial title shows the field and runway. Valdoro is a grass strip with no ILS. The violet marker on the heading card points to the selected field.
